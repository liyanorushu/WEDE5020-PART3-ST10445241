Who We Are

Our Journey Since 2024

Sip & Sack Co. was founded in 2024 with a straightforward concept: commonplace objects like canvas tote bags, stainless-steel tumblers, and enamel badges should be as distinctive as the individuals who use them.

When Sip & Sack Co. was first established, it was a modest, hands-on store that sold bright enamel badges, sturdy canvas tote bags, and personalized stainless-steel tumblers. Initially, we handled each purchase by ourselves via emails, word-of-mouth, and social media posts.

Tracking orders and payments by hand proved too challenging as our products gained popularity outside of our local area. We opened our official online store to give our consumers in South Africa a quicker, safer, and simpler purchase experience. Custom design previews, safe payments, and automated ordering are all now available on our new website.

Our Core Purpose

Guided by quality, personality, and community growth.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, personalized products—such as sturdy stainless-steel tumblers and custom canvas tote bags—that allow students, young professionals, event planners, and local businesses to showcase their distinct personality and brand image.

Our Vision

To become the premier online retailer of personalized lifestyle products in South Africa, while expanding into eco-friendly printed goods and corporate wholesale merchandise.

Target Market

We serve local small businesses and event planners seeking personalized gifts and branded products, as well as young individuals, students, and working professionals between the ages of 18 and 35.
